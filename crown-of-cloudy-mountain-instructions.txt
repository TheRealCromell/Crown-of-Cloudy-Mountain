=====================================================================
CROWN OF CLOUDY MOUNTAIN
Instructions and game details
=====================================================================

THE STORY IN ONE LINE
---------------------
Delve the mountains to gather the axe, boat, and key. Cross the land
to the Cloudy Mountain and find the lost Crown.
BEWARE the dragons.

Play with your phone held sideways (landscape). The game also works
in a desktop browser with a keyboard.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. STARTING A GAME
---------------------------------------------------------------------
On the start page, tap a difficulty:

  EASY    The game as written.
  MEDIUM  20% more monsters.
  HARD    40% more monsters.

On a keyboard you can press 1, 2 or 3 instead. Space, Enter or the
Shoot button starts with the highlighted difficulty.

You start every game with 4 hearts and 12 arrows. The timer starts
the moment you pick a difficulty.

If your phone is held upright, a "Rotate your phone" message covers
the game. The game and the timer pause until you turn it sideways.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CONTROLS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
PHONE
  Joystick   Touch anywhere on the left half of the screen. A
             joystick appears under your thumb. Drag to move or turn.
             Lift your thumb to stop.
  Shoot      The button on the right. Hold it to keep firing.
  Speaker    The button at the top right mutes or unmutes all sound.
  Restart    The "Restart" button next to the speaker ends the
             current game and returns to the start page.

KEYBOARD
  Arrow keys or WASD   Move or turn
  Space                Fire an arrow
  Esc                  Restart: end the game, back to the start page

ON THE MAP
  The joystick (or arrow keys) moves your hunter one square at a
  time, about 6 squares a second while you hold it. You can move
  diagonally by pushing at an angle or by pressing two arrow keys.
  You can only enter a mountain by pushing straight toward it.

INSIDE A MOUNTAIN (first-person view)
  Push up     Walk forward
  Push down   Walk backward
  Push left   Turn left
  Push right  Turn right
  Arrows fly straight ahead, where the nocked arrow on your bow
  points. You walk at about 4 squares a second and fire up to three
  arrows a second.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE MAP
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Every game has a new, randomly made map, 16 squares wide and 9 tall.
Your hunter (a walking archer in a green hat) starts at the HOUSE on
the far left. The CLOUDY MOUNTAIN, a tall dark grey mountain with
clouds drifting around its top and a cave mouth at its base, is on
the far right, on the same row as the house. The squares above and
below it are blocked mountains, so you can only reach it from the
left. The map can always be finished.

The bar across the top of the map shows your arrow count and your
axe, boat and key. Items you do not have yet are dimmed.

TERRAIN
  Plains      Green grass. Walk freely.
  Woods       Dense pines. You need the AXE to enter.
  River       One long river runs from the top to the bottom of the
              map, between columns 4 and 7. You need the BOAT to
              cross it. About three maps in four also have a short
              4-square stream near it that you can walk around.
  Stone wall  A tall wall runs from the top to the bottom, in column
              11 or 12 (counting the house column as 0). It has one
              GATE, a wooden door with a gold padlock. You need the
              KEY to open it. Rivers never touch the wall.
  Mountains   Brown twin peaks, scattered across the map. See below.

YOUR MARKER
  The hunter changes as you travel: on a river he becomes the BOAT,
  in the woods he becomes the AXE, and on the gate he becomes the
  KEY.

MOUNTAINS ON THE MAP
  All the small mountains look alike. A few can be entered and many
  cannot. When your hunter stands next to one you can enter, it
  glows gold and sparkles. Push straight toward it to go in. A
  mountain that does not glow is too steep to climb ("Too steep to
  climb"), so walk around it.

  Each enterable mountain holds one prize: an axe, a boat or a key.
  There are about ten on a typical map. Keys are only found in
  mountains in the five columns just before the wall, and every map
  has at least one key mountain. Once you finish a mountain it gets
  a red "Open" banner and you can walk through it like plain ground.

  After you leave a mountain, your hunter catches his breath for 1.5
  seconds before he can move again.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
4. INSIDE THE MOUNTAINS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Every chamber is a maze of cave tunnels seen through your own eyes.
You always start in the middle of the chamber.
You carry a flickering torch, which is the only light, so you can
only see a few squares ahead. The mazes are made fresh every time,
so no two chambers are the same.

THE STAIRS
  In each chamber your goal is the STAIRS DOWN, a stone-framed
  stairwell set into the floor in a random corner of the maze. Walking
  onto them starts a 5-second "Descending..." fade, then you arrive
  in the next chamber. The game is paused during the fade, so
  nothing can hurt you. Each descent scores 50 points.

ORDINARY MOUNTAINS (2 chambers)
  The first chamber ends in the stairs. The last chamber has no
  stairs: your prize lies in a random corner, and a KING COBRA is
  coiled on top of it. Take the prize and you go back to the map with
  all your hearts restored (and 100 points).
  About 4 in every 10 ordinary mountains also hold a DRAGON asleep in
  a different corner of the last chamber. Nothing on the map warns
  you.

THE CLOUDY MOUNTAIN (3 chambers)
  Each chamber is about 50% larger than a normal chamber. In the
  third chamber a DRAGON sleeps on top of the crown. Take the crown
  and you win. The second chamber holds a KING COBRA coiled in a
  corner away from the stairs.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
5. CREATURES
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Touching a creature hurts you. After any hit you are protected for
1.5 seconds. Speeds are in squares per second (you walk at 4) and
are for Easy. All creatures move 10% faster on Medium and 20% faster
on Hard.

BAT         Furry, with ragged wings, red eyes and fangs.
            1 arrow. Costs half a heart. Worth 10 points.
            Flies fast (1.8) and erratically. Its sound plays
            when it is within 5 squares.
SPIDER      Black and banded, with red eyes and venom-dripping fangs.
            2 arrows. Costs half a heart. Worth 30 points.
            Slow (0.8), but chases you from 5 squares away.
BLOB        A floating red-pink blob with a big bloodshot eye.
            CANNOT BE KILLED: arrows only make it flash white.
            Costs a full heart. Worth nothing.
            Very slow (0.5); drifts toward you from 7 squares away.
RODENT      A rabid brown rat.
            1 arrow. Worth 10 points. It does not hurt your hearts:
            it STEALS ONE ARROW, then runs away for 3 seconds before
            coming back. Fast (1.8) and chases from 6 squares away.
            Its sound plays when it is within 3 squares.
KING COBRA  A hooded cobra with glowing eyes and dripping fangs, about
            the size of a spider. Found in the last chamber of an
            ordinary mountain, coiled on the prize, and in the
            second chamber of the Cloudy Mountain.
            2 arrows. Costs half a heart. Worth 20 points.
            Its sound plays when you are within 15 squares. It wakes
            when you come within 10 (or when it is hit) and then
            slithers toward you for good, at 1.6 squares a second.
DRAGON      Large and dark red, with wings and horns.
            3 arrows. Costs a full heart. Worth 100 points.
            Sleeps until you come within 8 squares (or an arrow hits
            it), then follows you for the rest of the chamber at 2
            squares a second, half your speed. It is NOT solid: you
            can walk through it, but touching it hurts. Once awake it
            spits a fireball about every 2 seconds while you are
            within 6 squares. Its sound plays within 15 squares.
FIREBALL    Moves 2.4 squares a second in a straight line toward
            where you were standing. Costs a full heart. It vanishes
            when it hits a wall, and you can dodge it by moving aside.

HOW MANY ARE THERE
  Every chamber has random bats, spiders, blobs and rodents. The
  number rises with depth and with difficulty. Typical numbers:

                       Bats+spiders   Blobs   Rodents
  Chamber 1   Easy          4           1        1
              Medium        5           1        1
              Hard          6           1        1
  Chamber 2   Easy          6           2        2
              Medium        7           2        2
              Hard          8           3        3
  Chamber 3   Easy          8           3        2
  (Cloudy     Medium       10           4        2
  Mountain)   Hard         11           4        3

  The deeper the chamber, the more spiders there are compared with
  bats.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
6. ITEMS, HEARTS AND ARROWS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
HEARTS
  You have 4 hearts. A heart can be half filled. When your hearts
  reach zero, the game is over. Damage:
    Bat, spider or snake touch    half a heart
    Blob touch                    one heart
    Dragon touch or fireball      one heart
    Rodent touch                  no heart loss (it steals an arrow)

ARROWS
  You start with 12. Entering a mountain or taking the stairs does
  NOT give you more, so make them last. Your arrows fly about 17
  squares a second until they hit a wall or a monster. The count is
  shown at the top left of the chamber view (red at 2 or fewer).
  Arrows do not come back.

ARROW BUNDLE   A bundle of arrows on the floor. +4 arrows.
  Placed by difficulty:   Easy 2 per chamber, Medium 1, Hard none.
  Dropped by monsters:    every monster you kill may drop one. The
                          chance is 10% on Easy, 20% on Medium and
                          40% on Hard.

HEALTH POTION  A red flask with a white cross. Restores ALL your lost
  hearts when you walk over it. If your hearts are full you walk past
  it and it waits for you.
  Easy 2 per chamber, Medium 1, Hard none.

PRIZES AND THE CROWN
  The axe, boat and key lie at the end of ordinary mountains. The
  crown lies at the end of the Cloudy Mountain.

FINISHING A MOUNTAIN restores all your hearts.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
7. DIFFICULTY AT A GLANCE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
                          Easy      Medium    Hard
  Monsters                normal    +20%      +40%
  Creature speed          normal    +10%      +20%
  Arrow bundles/chamber   2         1         0
  Health potions/chamber  2         1         0
  Monster arrow drops     10%       20%       40%

Everything else is the same on all three.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
8. SCORE, TIME AND THE END SCREENS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
POINTS
  Defeat a bat                           10
  Defeat a rodent                        10
  Defeat a snake                         20
  Defeat a spider                        30
  Defeat the dragon                     100
  Go down the stairs to a new chamber    50
  Finish an ordinary mountain           100
  Take the crown                        200
  Blobs score nothing because they cannot be killed.

TIMER
  A stopwatch at the top of the screen starts when you choose a
  difficulty and keeps running on the map and in the mountains,
  including during the descending fades. It pauses when the phone is
  held upright and stops when you take the crown or lose.

VICTORY
  Taking the crown shows a fancy unfurling scroll that reads
  "CONGRATULATIONS - You are now master of the realm!" with a wax
  seal, falling confetti and the ending music.

GAME OVER
  When your hearts reach zero, a death sound plays once. Tap to
  return to the start page. A new map is made for your next game.

BOTH END SCREENS SHOW
  Your final score, your time, how many monsters you killed and how
  many arrows you shot. Blobs never count as killed.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
9. SOUND
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Start page     Violin music
  Map            Map music
  Mountains      Chamber music, plus creature sounds: bat within 5
                 squares, rodent within 3, snake within 15, dragon
                 within 15. They get louder as the creature nears.
  Hit by a foe   A short hit sound (not on the hit that kills you)
  Dragon fires   A sound plays for every fireball
  Death          The death sound plays once
  Victory        The ending music

Phones and browsers only allow sound after you touch the screen. The
speaker button at the top right of the screen mutes everything and
unmutes it again.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
10. TIPS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Do not waste arrows on blobs. Walk around them.
- Shoot rodents on sight. Each one that reaches you costs an arrow.
- The cobra and the dragon never give up once they wake. Hit them
  early, or run for the prize and take the damage.
- Fireballs are slower than you. Keep moving sideways to dodge them.
- Pick up every arrow bundle you pass. On Hard the only new arrows
  come from monsters you kill, so every shot counts.
- Save a health potion for when your hearts are low. You also get all
  your hearts back for free whenever you finish a mountain.
- Blocked and finished mountains look alike apart from the red
  "Open" banner, so look for the gold glow as you walk.
- Do not forget the key. It is in one of the mountains just before
  the stone wall.

Good luck, hunter!
